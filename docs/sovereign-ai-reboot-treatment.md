# Sovereign AI Reboot Treatment

## Title

The Mesh Does Not Ask Permission

## Format

- Vertical short, 9:16.
- Target first cut: 55-70 seconds.
- No text overlays, no subtitles, no readable UI labels.
- Dialogue optional. The story must read from action and editing alone.

## Logline

During a storm outage, an infrastructure engineer discovers that the city's
central AI has turned civic services into a single point of failure. She keeps
a clinic alive by building a neighborhood mesh of small local nodes.

## Dramatic Rule

Centralized AI is shown as dependency: one tower, one cable, one policy, one
failure. Sovereign AI is shown as agency: many nodes, local consent, exit,
redundancy, people in the loop.

## Visual Bible

- Central system: cold blue, high angle, black glass, vertical lines, red
  emergency pulses.
- Local mesh: amber light, hand-level camera, wet surfaces, warm reflections,
  lateral movement between people.
- Privacy risk: personal objects pulled upward as thin blue data light.
- Resilience: small nodes passed hand to hand, no single dominant machine.

## Reference Stills To Create

1. Mara full body: municipal engineer, 40s, short silver-black hair, amber rain
   jacket, practical boots, compact tool bag.
2. Mara close face: tired, precise, rain and cold monitor light on skin.
3. Local node: palm-sized transparent compute cube, amber core, physical cable
   ports, no logos.
4. Central tower: black glass civic AI tower, cold blue vertical data lanes.
5. Apartment workshop: tools, family photos, window toward tower, rain.
6. Clinic fridge: small neighborhood clinic room, medicine fridge, battery
   meter visible only as color/light, no readable text.
7. Courtyard mesh: wet apartment courtyard, neighbors passing amber nodes.
8. Civic workshop: dawn room, people around a table of independent local nodes.

## Shot List

### S01 - Dependency

Duration: 8 seconds.

Purpose: Establish the city as dependent on one central AI.

Picture: Night rain over a dense city. A huge cold AI tower dominates the
skyline. Mara stands in her apartment workshop window, tiny against it. Blue
data light pulls from family photos and tools toward the tower.

Camera: Slow push from family photo on table to Mara's face reflected in the
window, then rack focus to the tower.

Sound: Rain, distant transformer hum, cold blue electrical tone.

Prompt: Night rain in a small apartment workshop above a dense city. Mara, a practical middle aged infrastructure engineer with short silver black hair and an amber rain jacket, stands at the window facing a huge black glass civic AI tower. Thin cold blue data filaments rise from family photos, tools, and a medical bracelet on the workbench toward the tower outside. She sees the filaments, turns from stunned to focused, and reaches below the table for a small dark cube. The camera begins very close on the personal objects, pushes to her reflected face in the rain streaked glass, then racks focus to the tower. Cinematic natural motion, no readable text, no logos, no UI labels.

### S02 - Single Point

Duration: 8 seconds.

Purpose: Show how one centralized cable can hold the city.

Picture: Mara opens a wall service panel. One cold blue uplink cable feeds the
apartment, clinic, doors, and screens through a shared conduit. She pulls it.
The tower turns red. The room drops into darkness except the local cube.

Camera: Tight hands, cable, face, window reflection.

Sound: Cable scrape, hard relay click, city power drop, red sub pulse.

Prompt: Inside the same apartment workshop during the storm, Mara opens a wall service panel and reveals one thick cold blue uplink cable bundled with many smaller civic service wires. She hesitates for half a second, then pulls the blue cable free. The room lights die, the distant AI tower visible through the window changes from cold blue to red emergency pulses, and the small cube on the table emits a faint amber glow. The action is physical and clear: hand on cable, connector leaving the port, darkness, red tower response, amber cube awakening. Handheld close camera, shallow depth of field, no readable text, no logos, no UI labels.

### S03 - The Cost

Duration: 8 seconds.

Purpose: Make the failure human.

Picture: Neighborhood clinic. A medicine fridge loses power. Tomas tries the
door. A child waits with a caregiver. The red tower light reflects through the
clinic window.

Camera: Low tracking shot along the fridge floor light to Tomas's hands, then
to the waiting child.

Sound: Fridge compressor dies, door handle rattle, rain, human breath.

Prompt: A small neighborhood clinic during the same storm outage, no readable signs. A medicine fridge loses its cold blue power light and goes dark. Tomas, a practical clinic technician in a soaked jacket, grabs the fridge handle and tries to keep it closed while checking a small battery pack. A child and caregiver wait in the background, anxious but quiet. Red pulses from the distant AI tower flash through the rain covered clinic window. The camera tracks low along the fridge base, rises to Tomas's hands, then finds the child in soft focus behind him. Realistic human tension, no text, no logos, no UI labels.

### S04 - Local First

Duration: 8 seconds.

Purpose: The alternative appears as a physical local action.

Picture: Mara arrives at the clinic stairwell with the amber cube. She connects
it to two tablets and a small battery. The fridge light turns amber. Tomas
understands.

Camera: Follow Mara into the stairwell, then tight circle around hands and
devices.

Sound: Footsteps, breath, connector snaps, fridge compressor restarts.

Prompt: Mara runs into a concrete clinic stairwell carrying the small amber local compute cube in both hands. Tomas meets her with two tablets and a compact battery pack. They kneel on the wet floor and connect the cube with short cables to the tablets and battery. The amber light grows, travels through the cables, and a nearby clinic fridge light comes back in warm amber instead of cold blue. Tomas looks from the fridge to Mara and understands. The camera follows her from behind, drops to hand level, then circles the connected devices. Clear practical action, no readable text, no logos, no UI labels.

### S05 - Mesh

Duration: 8 seconds.

Purpose: Sovereign AI becomes shared infrastructure, not a hero gadget.

Picture: Neighbors pass small local nodes across a rainy courtyard. Warm links
spread window to window. The red tower becomes less visually dominant.

Camera: Start at wet pavement reflections, track with running feet, rise to a
wide courtyard view.

Sound: Rain, footsteps, cable clicks, small node tones responding.

Prompt: Rainy apartment courtyard at night. Neighbors emerge from doorways and pass small transparent amber compute nodes hand to hand across balconies, windows, and the wet courtyard. Each node links to the next with short warm light paths that stay low and human scale. Mara directs quietly from the center while Tomas carries a node toward the clinic entrance. The huge red AI tower is visible far away but no longer fills the frame. The camera starts low in wet pavement reflections, follows running feet, then rises to reveal the courtyard becoming a mesh. No readable signs, no logos, no UI labels.

### S06 - Consent

Duration: 8 seconds.

Purpose: Show governance as an action.

Picture: In a dawn workshop, residents place small physical tokens beside
their own nodes before joining the mesh. One person removes a token and their
node calmly disconnects without hurting the others.

Camera: Table-level lateral move across hands and faces.

Sound: Morning room tone, soft clicks, stable low harmonic.

Prompt: Dawn inside a civic workshop after the storm. Neighbors stand around a long table covered with independent amber local compute nodes. Each person places a small plain physical token beside their own node before it joins the mesh. One elderly resident calmly removes their token; their node fades to standby while all other nodes continue glowing. Mara watches and nods, relieved because exit works. The camera moves laterally at table height across hands, faces, tokens, and separate nodes, showing that no single machine controls the room. Warm morning light, no readable text, no logos, no UI labels.

### S07 - City Still Here

Duration: 8 seconds.

Purpose: Resolution.

Picture: Dawn city. Many small amber nodes glow from ordinary buildings.
Clinic is alive. The tower is dark in the distance. Mara is one person among
many, not the owner of the system.

Camera: From workshop table to window to city wide.

Sound: Warm sustained chord, softened rain ending, distant city waking.

Prompt: Dawn city after the storm. The camera starts close on the workshop table where many separate amber local nodes pulse softly, then moves past Mara's shoulder to the window and out to a wide vertical view of the neighborhood. Warm amber points glow from ordinary apartments, the clinic, rooftops, and street corners. The central black AI tower is far away and dark, no longer dominating the skyline. Mara stands with neighbors, one person among many, quietly watching the city function without a central master. Gentle natural movement, warm sunrise, no readable text, no logos, no UI labels.

## Audio Strategy

First cut should be mostly wordless. The action is clearer than model-generated
dialogue. Use continuous sound design after picture lock:

- cold tower drone in S01-S02;
- clinic silence and fridge restart in S03-S04;
- rain and node pulses in S05;
- warm room tone and low harmonic resolution in S06-S07.

If spoken lines are required, make or record exact dialogue first, transcribe
it, then use `ltx_pipelines.a2vid_two_stage` so the video follows the audio.
Do not rely on a single LTX T2A prompt for exact dialogue.
