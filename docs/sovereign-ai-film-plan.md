# Sovereign AI Short Film Plan

## Working Title

**The Last Central Server**

## Format

- Vertical short film, 9:16, 1080x1920.
- Target length: 50-60 seconds.
- No text overlays, no titles inside the picture, no logos, no UI labels.
- The story must read through camera, light, rhythm, sound, and repeated motifs.

## Logline

In a rain-soaked city dependent on one vast AI tower, a single local node becomes the seed of a quieter, more resilient intelligence.

## Dramatic Argument

Centralized AI is not evil because it looks powerful; it is risky because it makes everyone dependent on one owner, one policy, one outage, and one extraction point. Sovereign AI is not a gadget fantasy; it is the human-scale alternative: local agency, verifiable compute, shared resilience.

## Visual Grammar

- **Centralized system:** cold blue, black glass, high angles, symmetry, vertical dominance, searchlights, cables pulling upward.
- **Human agency:** warm amber, hand-level camera, shallow focus, natural surfaces, breath, rain, windows.
- **Fragility:** red light, discontinuity, failed connections, heavy negative space, stuttered motion.
- **Sovereign mesh:** dawn, gold paths, lateral movement, many small sources, no single center.

## Recurring Motifs

- A warm local core in human hands.
- A cold tower that never shows a face.
- Data streams rising away from people.
- Cables and light paths: first extractive, then cooperative.
- Rain ending in sunrise.

## Shot List

1. **Cold city dependency.** Rooftop wide: the protagonist faces the central AI tower while holding a dim local core.
2. **Watcher insert.** Extreme telephoto detail of tower windows and searchlights scanning the city.
3. **Extraction chamber.** Interior of the centralized system, private data streams rising into a black sphere.
4. **Human cost insert.** Apartment close-up: a sleeping face and family photos lit by cold reflected data light, suggesting private life being abstracted.
5. **The local node wakes.** Warm desk-level shot as a small AI node stabilizes in an apartment workshop.
6. **Hands decide.** Close-up of hands disconnecting a cloud uplink cable and moving it to a local compute port.
7. **System failure.** The central tower turns emergency red in the storm; city connections drop.
8. **Outage on the ground.** Street-level silhouettes under dead screens, no panic, only sudden dependence made visible.
9. **First neighbor.** The protagonist carries the warm node through a stairwell; light spills under doors.
10. **Mesh ignition.** Dawn rooftop/city wide: many small local nodes begin linking across ordinary buildings.
11. **Civic room.** Human-scale public compute modules glow in a sunlit workshop or library.
12. **Final image.** Close human circle around the shared local compute lattice, no tower dominating the skyline.

## Soundtrack Plan

- **Act 1:** low sub drone, rain, distant transformers, cold high partials.
- **Act 2:** interior suction and data noise, glassy resonances, muffled heart-like pulse.
- **Act 3:** warm analog synth emerges with the local node, tactile clicks, room tone.
- **Act 4:** red outage swell, dropouts, low impact without explosion.
- **Act 5:** dawn harmonic bed, softened city air, restrained pulse pattern spreading node to node.

The soundtrack should not behave like stock trailer music. It should feel designed: sparse, physical, slightly ominous at first, then gradually warmer.
